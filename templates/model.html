They are not measured here, and this prototype does not support them. The measured numbers above are what the code produces, and this page reads them from metrics.json so it cannot drift from the training run.

Known limits

  • {{ m.dataset.records }} records from one park over four years. Nothing here has been validated on other regions, sensors or seasons.
  • The burned-area label mixes tiny and huge events into one "fire" class, and the inputs are weather indices only (no vegetation, ignition sources or human activity).
  • CNN (satellite imagery) and LSTM (time series) models from the disclosure are not implemented: the prototype dataset has neither images nor sequences.
  • {{ m.dataset.duplicates }} exact duplicate rows are kept, as in the original dataset; a random split can put a copy on each side.
